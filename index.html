Travel FAQs

Everything you need to know about visiting Hargeisa.

What is the local currency?
The local currency is the Somaliland Shilling (SLSH). However, US Dollars are widely accepted everywhere, and most people use mobile money (ZAAD or eDahab) for daily transactions.
Is it safe to visit Hargeisa?
Yes, Somaliland is known to be very safe and peaceful for travelers. Locals are incredibly hospitable and welcoming to tourists. Basic common sense travel precautions are advised.
When is the best time to visit?
The best time to visit is during the cooler months, generally from October to March. Summers can get quite warm.
What language is spoken?
Somali is the official language. Arabic and English are also widely spoken, especially in business, cafes, and by the younger generation.
